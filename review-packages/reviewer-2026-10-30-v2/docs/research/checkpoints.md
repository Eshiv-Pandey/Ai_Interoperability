# Evidence-based research checkpoints

Started 2026-10-24. These weights were defined before experiments; they measure completed deliverables, not chance of acceptance or novelty. Only a fully passed gate earns its weight. In-progress implementation is described without fractional credit. The registered title and approved study scope are preserved.

| Checkpoint | Weight | Cumulative after passing | Required evidence |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| CP1: Frozen scope and testable foundation | 5% | 5% | Hashed approved-scope snapshot; explicit finite semantics; established baseline and separately authored oracle; positive/negative/unknown and forged-certificate tests; actual development traces; clean local replay instructions |
| CP2: Novelty and real-route feasibility | 20% | 25% | Revalidated closest sources; two eligible independently implemented pinned bridges with actual wire traces and native control; demonstrated candidate advantage against same-evidence exact/credible contract comparators; recorded human novelty/feasibility review |
| CP3: Benchmark and measurement validity | 20% | 45% | Two resettable application-style domains; development/calibration/held-out corpus and independent runtime/gold; reviewed reference trajectories and near misses; validated graders/trace fields; fixed actual crossing placements, baselines and ablations |
| CP4: Pilot and confirmation freeze | 10% | 55% | Measured local pilot and integrity checks; verified resources and atomic usage ledger if any live study; prospectively chosen sample/settings, sealed holdout, analysis/retry/exclusion policy and exact run manifest |
| CP5: Complete frozen experiments | 20% | 75% | All prespecified local and applicable live attempts with immutable raw records; strong comparisons, ablations, crossing counts and source/result ledger; no omitted failures or favorable stopping |
| CP6: Independent reproduction and analysis | 10% | 85% | Full local clean-environment replay; independent headline witnesses/claims; prespecified fresh live subset if applicable; paired uncertainty and dependency sensitivity; documented adverse scientific review |
| CP7: Complete paper and defensibility audit | 15% | 100% | Full manuscript, bibliography and trace-derived figures/tables; claim-to-source/result audit; artifact setup/limitations; accountable human review, authorship and AI-use disclosures; publication-readiness decision |

Status is recorded in [checkpoint-status.json](checkpoint-status.json), with links to exact evidence. A failed novelty/bridge gate cannot be marked passed by renaming the project or silently removing requirements. Conditional omission of live inference is permitted by v1 only when no reliable verified access exists, and restricts claims accordingly. A paid run cannot compensate for missing novelty or independent validation.

CP1 independent implementation means separate logic authored in separate tasks; it does **not** imply independent human validation. CP2/CP3/CP6 human-review requirements stay pending until actual reviewers provide recorded assessments. Synthetic/constructed fixtures are explicitly development inputs, not real bridge failure data.

Conference acceptance, public release, submission and proceedings publication are subsequent milestones. None is implied by reaching 100% of this research-and-paper-draft plan.
