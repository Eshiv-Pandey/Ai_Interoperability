Research manuscript, version 14, 11 November 2026. This successor preserves the
audited V11 manuscript, lossless V2 proof codec and historical failed attempts,
and integrates the actual separately retained V13 development allocation.
Earlier V11 matrix execution was blocked by failed smoke verification; V12

## Abstract

An accepted protocol message need not preserve the operations required to finish
an agent's original task. We implement bounded task-relative continuation
admission with three-valued decisions and independently checked positive and
complete-model negative certificates. A shared-lifting adaptation caches exact
prefixes and complete relation expansions while preserving correlated binding
frames, uncertainty and witness history. The original four-method development
matrix contains 66 authored profiles at four declared work allowances. A
separate conventional constraint-pruning experiment adds a fifth method on
identical profiles, producing 1,320 analyzed cells. At allowance 256, shared
lifting certifies 39/66 profiles; both goal-pruned and constraint-pruned direct
search certify 54/66. At allowance 100,000, all five methods certify the same
54 profiles. On a reconvergence stress case, relation checks fall from 1,024
to 16, but charged work rises from 5,566 to 14,773 and the 8,895-byte proof is
unchanged. A separately audited Linux reproduction matches the original
deterministic matrix. Original AgentOps confirmation tasks complete through
native and admitted bridge routes with one attributed effect each. A separate
original AP2 development sequence resumes its merchant and processor tasks to
a corresponding demo Success receipt under offline-model and ASGI interventions.
A subsequent shared-lifting-gated AP2 continuation is checked before its actual
send and reaches a separately verified demo receipt. These results establish
reproducible adverse evidence for the implemented cache
and proof representation, and separate model-relative admission from actual
task effects and observation completeness. A separate 1,584-cell cache
ablation supplies no additional certified cases over goal-pruned direct. A
65-cell original AP2 payment-tool development comparison observes 3/13 demo
purchases with admission alone versus 10/13 with informed public recovery;
36 purchase observations include five subsequent duplicate-call errors. The
original ADK shopper allocation observes 29/29 prefixes but zero completed
purchases, preserving the original missing-challenge boundary. These offline
development observations do not establish algorithmic
superiority, native AP2 qualification or held-out live-model generalization.
The earlier V11 owned-TCP matrix remained unexecuted after a failed smoke
capture; all 104 matrix observations are missing and all 65 native transport
pairs are non-evaluable. V12 failed during constructed controls before actual
allocation, so it supplies no matrix or transport-pair census. A separate
retained V13 allocation verifies 65/65 native payment-tool captures and
39/39 repeated legal bridge initiation captures, retaining every adverse
outcome. Owned TCP serving does not establish original native-launcher,
provider, whole-task or human-gold qualification.

A separate common-cost development allocation retains all 1,650 attempts;
each method has 270 captured certified results and 60 unknown results. All ten
paired comparisons contain 54 profiles with five dependent timing repeats.
Shared lifting adds no certified coverage and has higher observed full-process
CPU, wall and RSS medians than goal-pruned direct on every eligible profile.
These descriptive costs establish no novelty or held-out superiority.

The lossless V2 proof codec restores all 26 retained negative certificates;
complete-wrapper bytes change from 15,128 to 11,918, versus 7,760 for direct.
Two encoded proofs shrink and 24 grow. A separate hosted reproduction passes
31 constructed codec controls. These are representation and reproducibility
observations without a new solver, checker-speed or novelty claim.

## 1. Introduction

Agent communication protocols expose messages, tasks, resources and optional
extensions. These interfaces enable integration, but interoperability is a
property of a task and its route, rather than of message syntax alone. A caller
may successfully send a message while lacking the reference-bearing operation
needed to answer a pending confirmation. An adapter may retain a usable local
handle while the authoritative task remains unfinished. A completed task can
also fail the intended application requirement if the wrong target changed or
the action was duplicated. These outcomes require different measurements.

This work asks whether a receiving caller has at least one permitted completion
within an explicit horizon. The input separates an abstract route from the
public argument recipes and request relations needed to realize that route.
A positive answer supplies a replayable witness. A negative answer requires
complete relevant finite evidence and an exhaustion certificate. When freshness,
coverage, interpretation or available work is insufficient, the method returns
unknown. Unknown is retained as lost certified coverage rather than counted as
a correct rejection.

The implemented candidate follows witness lifting and exact-prefix refinement,
a bounded adaptation of established counterexample-guided and lazy symbolic
reasoning. Its shared-lifting successor adds ordinary memoization. The motivating
observation is that reconvergent abstract paths can repeat the same concrete
relation expansions. However, reducing this repetition does not necessarily
reduce the cost of indexing, maintaining path histories, constructing
certificates or checking them. A fair direct search can also avoid expanding
branches that cannot reach a goal within the remaining horizon. The empirical
question is therefore whether cache reuse improves the complete admission
pipeline against that stronger comparator.

Our development study gives an adverse answer for the implemented shared-lifting
representation. Shared lifting adds no certified profiles beyond goal-pruned
direct search at any of the four tested allowances. At the largest allowance,
all methods agree on 28 supported, 26 unsupported and 12 unknown profiles, while
the shared implementation uses substantially more of its declared work units.
The most informative stress case reduces relation evaluations without reducing
the explicit path-list proof. This is a concrete implementation result, not a
general impossibility theorem for symbolic methods.

The paper reports three completed components: a finite admission and certificate
artifact, a same-input four-method comparison and separately executed fifth comparator
with raw evidence and cross-platform
deterministic reproduction, and an original-application confirmation fixture in
which a conditional admitted continuation is followed by the required task
completion and effect. These components preserve the project's broader intended
method and two-domain evaluation scope. They do not establish that the original
novelty or application-validity gates have been met. No first-composition,
first-handoff-measurement or new-mathematical-machinery claim is made.

## 2. Related work and comparator requirements

Protocol composition and runtime admissibility already have formal treatments.
AgentThread's composition analysis [1], CONTINUITY's security-context contracts
[2] and proof-carrying contracts [3] motivate explicit obligations, bindings and
evidence boundaries. Their properties and quantifiers differ from the bounded
existential completion target here. Replacing a universal security property with
a may-completion question is a change of specification, not sufficient evidence
of an algorithmic contribution.

Recovery and handoff studies likewise constrain the interpretation of this
artifact. BRIDGE [4] and AgentAsk [5] provide relevant boundary-repair,
clarification and capable-peer alternatives. Facts Without Rules [6] studies
operational information preservation in multi-agent handoffs. An empirical
comparison must give each arm the same permitted observations and recovery
alternatives. A practical admission policy cannot receive evaluator-private
answers that its comparator lacks. This study does not reproduce those systems
or compare live downstream utility against their released implementations.

Classical predicate transformers [7], assume-guarantee contract quotient [8],
input/output conformance and refinement [9], and Pacti [10] provide established
relational and contract reasoning. The direct product search used here belongs
to that broad finite-state lineage. Goals, task identifiers, caller principals
and expiry conditions are explicit state or contract variables; their presence
does not establish new theory.

Costly observation selection has further close comparators in equivalence-class
determination [11] and overlapping decision regions [12]. Their results depend
on particular observation, prior and decision assumptions. We do not transfer
those guarantees to an arbitrary bridge or claim a demonstrated acquisition
advantage. In the present finite benchmark, all methods receive the same supplied
profile rather than choosing different external observations.

Fresh-name and symbolic protocol methods are relevant when identifiers and
public history determine legal future requests. Fresh labelled transition
systems [13], YAPA [16], DEEPSEC [17] and symbolic partial-order reduction [18]
already reason about name histories, public recipes, frames, equality or action
dependencies under stated restrictions. Retaining whole correlated frames is
necessary here because pooling values from incompatible histories can invent a
completion. The current cache is an engineering mechanism within a supplied
finite relation model; it is not a general identifier quotient, a new reduction
theorem or a replacement for those analyses.

The software profiles include pinned a2a-to-mcp [14] and mcp-a2a [15] sources.
Their interfaces and documented omissions define different admissible route
profiles. A method must retain those distinctions rather than weaken the task
contract to make every route eligible. The strongest executed same-input
comparator in the present development study is goal-pruned memoized direct
search, rather than an abstraction-only feature check or an intentionally
uncached implementation.

The refreshed primary-source assessment adds the original CEGAR [19], relation
consistency [20], memo-functions [21] and bounded-model-checking [22] references.
The candidate follows the established abstract-path/concrete-check/refinement
pattern; the shared cache instantiates ordinary memoization. Constraint pruning
rejects partial assignments only after required guard dependencies are known
and a guard is definitely false. It does not implement AC-3 propagation or a
general CSP solver. The horizon bounds modeled steps; the work allowance can
instead cause unknown. None of these adaptations establishes a new general
verification principle. The bounded source assessment refreshed 22 identities
and retained failed retrievals and metadata gaps. It is an AI assessment, not an
exhaustive systematic review, prior-system reproduction or human contribution
approval.

## 3. Task-relative continuation semantics

### 3.1. Supplied route and recipe model

A profile has schema `recipe-continuation/1`. It contains an inner
`finite-continuation/1` route model, an initial public binding frame, one relation
per abstract transition, and an explicit finite-recipe coverage declaration.
The inner model supplies states, transitions, initial state, horizon, runtime
contract and evidence interval. Malformed input is rejected rather than
interpreted as interoperability failure.

Let S be the finite abstract state set, s0 the initial state, E the labelled
transitions, H the nonnegative transition horizon, I the invariant and G the
goal. A public frame f maps binding names to JSON scalars. For an edge e, the
supplied local relation R_e(f) enumerates legal argument recipes, emitted
requests, guards and atomic updates. A concrete modeled step is
`(s, f) --(e, args, request)--> (s', f')`. Relations have finite argument domains.
Public recipes copy an available binding or use a supplied literal. They cannot
read evaluator-private values or silently set a transport header absent from
the accepted caller interface.

Expressions construct requests from constants, arguments, old bindings, arrays
and objects; guards may inspect the emitted request. Updates read the old frame
and current request atomically. Frames are retained as complete assignments,
not independent sets of available values. Equality is structural and
type-sensitive: a boolean and integer of the same apparent value are distinct.
Explicit null, an absent emitted path and an unknown value have different
semantics. A definitely false conjunct rejects an assignment even if another
conjunct is indeterminate. Otherwise unknown data needed to construct or accept
a request prevents a concrete positive step.

The may-completion question is whether some trace of at most H modeled steps
starts at `(s0, f0)`, satisfies I at every visited state and reaches a state
satisfying G. An initial goal can have a zero-step witness. H counts modeled
transitions, not CPU instructions or a wall-clock service deadline. Repeated
states at different depths remain distinct with respect to the remaining
horizon.

### 3.2. Three-valued admission

| Decision | Justification within the accepted model |
| --- | --- |
| Supported | At least one definitely legal, invariant-safe bounded public-recipe witness reaches a definitely satisfied goal. |
| Unsupported | Fresh, complete relevant finite coverage and known contract/relation semantics exclude every legal safe goal path within the horizon. |
| Unknown | Stale or incomplete evidence, relevant indeterminacy, unresolved recipes or exhausted work prevents a justified conclusive result. |

A known witness may be supported in a partial model or when an alternative
assignment is unknown. Refuting all known paths in a partial model cannot
establish unsupported. The implementation retains a conservative
contracted-observable pass, including potentially safe reachable branches; it
does not claim maximal three-valued decision coverage. A checker rejection is
an uncertified result, never a supported or unsupported benchmark success.

The evaluation clock is an explicit integer and must lie within the supplied
evidence interval. This checks the declared interval; it does not establish a
service-issued lease, atomic multi-peer snapshot or immunity to future change.
Profile digests bind certificates to bytes, but a digest and a `source_ref`
string do not authenticate source acquisition or prove model fidelity.

### 3.3. Model-relative claims and deployment obligations

Two obligations connect a supplied model to a real route. For positive lifting,
every certified modeled step must correspond continuously to a legal concrete
request and outcome with the same task, context, principal, bindings and safety
monitor. Individually realizable edges are insufficient if their intermediate
bindings disagree. For concrete negative completeness, every legal completing
real trace within the declared bound must have a represented safe modeled
trace. An omitted public read, string guess, alternate operation, expiry event
or recovery route can invalidate that second obligation.

The finite software results assume those obligations have been accepted for the
supplied profile. They do not compile an arbitrary bridge automatically or prove
full action-space closure for the application. In particular, an API accepting
arbitrary strings cannot be replaced by a finite literal dictionary without
declaring the resulting restricted claim. A UUID-looking value does not itself
prove that a legal guess is impossible.

May completion also differs from a uniform caller guarantee. One successful
trace from a fixed state does not prove a policy that succeeds from every state
compatible with public evidence or against every environment outcome. Actual
language-model compliance is another question. The present methods certify
bounded existential witnesses, not unrestricted progress, universal safety of
all agent outputs or successful live-model behavior.

## 4. Admission algorithms and certificates

### 4.1. Same-input direct methods

Legacy relational direct search explores finite products of abstract state,
depth and complete binding frame. It is the historical control and disclosed
reference implementation. Its original goal-cone pass handled relevance of
uncertainty but did not prune all definitely dead forward relation expansions.
Using that incomplete optimization as the decisive comparator would overstate
a goal-first candidate's advantage on off-goal cases.

The goal-pruned successor preserves correlated frames, relation/frame
memoization, lazy goal-edge enumeration, freshness and coverage rules. Before
enumerating a relation's arguments, it suppresses that expansion only when the
target lies outside the possible-goal cone for the remaining horizon. The full
topology analysis and inspected outgoing edges retain their declared charges.
The resulting direct negative proof explicitly uses
`goal-pruned-relational-exhaustion/2`; it is not relabelled as the legacy product
closure. Its checker reconstructs the goal cone and exact relevant product
layers without calling the solver.

All five experimental methods receive the same refined recipe profile. A separately
labelled abstraction-only control is reported in Section 7.4, outside both
full-recipe experiments. That control receives the inner graph and answers the
weaker question of graph reachability without legal public request construction;
it does not replace the fair direct comparator.

### 4.2. Original witness lifting and refinement

Original refinement enumerates bounded abstract goal paths and tries to lift
each path from the initial public frame. Lifting propagates complete correlated
frames through the supplied relations while maintaining witness history and
uncertainty. A definitely empty exact prefix refutes every extension of that
prefix. The refinement trie records those prefixes, avoiding the unsound step
of disabling an edge globally after one binding context fails.

Consider two paths reaching the same abstract state. One yields
`{call: call-A, target: B}`, the other `{call: call-B, target: B}`. If the final
guard requires `call-B` and `B`, failure of the first path cannot refute the
second. If the second target were `A`, pooling `call-B` from that frame with `B`
from the first would invent a nonexistent positive witness. Exact frames and
exact-prefix refinements prevent both errors within the modeled semantics.

The candidate is a bounded CEGAR/lazy symbolic adaptation. The implemented
refinement machinery is concrete and executable; algorithmic distinctness from
the established lineage remains a separate scientific question.

The separately executed constraint-pruned direct successor uses the same product
search and proof checker as its disclosed direct baseline, but enumerates
argument domains with conservative dependency-aware guard pruning. Only a
definitely false guard whose argument dependencies have already been assigned
can suppress a partial branch. Complete surviving tuples pass through the
unchanged relation interpreter. Unknown guards do not justify suppression.
Its additional indexing and guard work are charged explicitly. The experiment
tests a conventional optimization, not a new relation-consistency algorithm.

### 4.3. Shared lifting

Shared lifting retains original abstract path enumeration, goal-first lifting,
refinement trie and three-valued rules. It introduces two caches local to one
validated invocation. The prefix cache is keyed by the exact transition-ID
tuple. A complete prefix summary stores its endpoint, depth, correlated frontier
with a valid representative history, sticky uncertainty and known-contract
state. The relation cache is keyed by transition ID and the canonical whole
before-frame. A complete relation summary stores distinct known successor
frames, one local concrete step per successor, and any indeterminate-assignment
flag.

Canonical keys use compact sorted JSON and retain every binding and scalar
type. Relation entries contain local steps, not a history from some other
prefix. Replaying a relation appends its step to the currently selected prefix's
history. Equal endpoint frames on different paths do not merge the prefix
histories or their horizon. Both caches are discarded at invocation end; they
do not authorize reuse across changed profiles, contracts, evidence intervals
or application states.

Algorithm 1 summarizes the complete-entry and uncertainty conditions. It omits
encoding and metering details but retains the implemented control decisions.

```text
ADMIT-SHARED(profile P, clock t, allowance B)
    validate P; reject malformed input
    if t lies outside P's evidence interval: return UNKNOWN
    initialize meter, exact-prefix cache, relation cache and refutation trie
    conservatively inspect bounded potentially safe contract observables
    enqueue (initial state, empty abstract path)
    while an abstract path remains:
        charge path/state and refinement-trie operations
        discard paths extending an exactly refuted prefix
        if the endpoint may satisfy the goal:
            disposition := LIFT(path)
            if disposition contains a known safe witness:
                materialize and return SUPPORTED with recipe-path certificate
            if disposition is definitely refuted:
                record its exact empty prefix in the refutation trie
                continue
            record unresolved uncertainty
        enqueue permitted potentially safe edges while depth < horizon
    if allowance was exhausted, relevant observables are unknown,
       a lifting result is unresolved, or coverage is incomplete:
        return UNKNOWN without a certificate
    return UNSUPPORTED with complete refinement-exhaustion certificate

LIFT(exact path)
    retrieve the longest completed exact-prefix summary, or start at f0
    for each remaining edge and each correlated before-frame:
        retrieve its complete relation summary if present
        otherwise enumerate legal finite assignments using original semantics
        on a final known-safe goal edge, return the first known legal witness
        publish a relation entry only after complete expansion and all charges
        append each local step to this prefix's own valid history
        retain complete distinct successor frames and sticky uncertainty
        publish a prefix entry only after complete expansion and all charges
        if no known successor remains:
            return UNKNOWN if uncertainty is sticky, otherwise exact REFUTATION
    return a known safe witness if one exists, otherwise UNKNOWN
```

An early witness or budget interruption never publishes an incomplete relation
entry. Proper-prefix summaries require complete expansion. A cached complete
relation can supply a concrete final step without repeating its assignment
search. A known safe witness can succeed despite uncertainty in another
assignment; an empty frontier with uncertainty cannot become a refutation.

### 4.4. Certificates, checking and declared cost

Supported `recipe-path` certificates contain the ordered transition IDs,
arguments, emitted requests and before/after frames. Independent replay checks
the profile digest, clock, horizon, legal recipes, guards, atomic updates,
continuous bindings, invariants and final goal. Original and shared refinement
use `refinement-exhaustion` negative certificates listing exact refuted
prefixes. The preserved refinement oracle re-lifts those prefixes from the
input and checks their cover of relevant bounded paths; it receives no trusted
solver cache. Direct exhaustion certificates instead represent product closure.
The explicit goal-pruned kind has its corresponding reconstructive checker.

The implementation has a separate admission adapter that records input bytes,
solver selection, result and checker acceptance. Its conclusive outcomes require
the checker to return exactly true. Unknown must have no certificate. Independent
workers isolate solver and checker execution in the benchmark, retaining raw
dispatches and replies. Independently authored checker logic is a software
independence boundary, not independent human scientific review or proof of a
faithful real-world projection.

Original work categories remain charged. Shared lifting additionally charges
cache lookup, canonical-key components, insertion, replayed frames and
successors, history construction and witness materialization. Payload nodes
copied or traversed are charged, and an empty hit is not free. Cache peak counts
are auxiliary storage observations rather than additional work ticks. Equal
numeric allowances limit each method's declared operation meter; they do not
equate hardware effort across different operation categories.

## 5. Properties under explicit assumptions

The following arguments concern the stated finite semantics and complete cache
summaries. They are proof sketches of the design obligations, not a mechanized
verification of all Python execution or a source-to-model compiler theorem.
Assume that the supplied profile remains unchanged during one call, finite
argument enumeration covers its declared domain, local expressions/guards/updates
have the specified deterministic semantics, canonical keys distinguish whole
typed frames, and each published cache summary is complete.

**Proposition 1: local relation reuse preserves modeled successor meaning.**
For a fixed edge and whole before-frame, the supplied relation has the same
legal assignments, emitted requests, guard results and atomic updates on every
evaluation in the call. Its completed summary therefore has exactly the known
successor frames and unresolved-assignment flag of a fresh complete expansion.
The transition and complete frame are both in the key, so results for a different
binding context cannot be substituted. Publishing only after complete expansion
prevents an interrupted prefix of the domain from becoming an exhaustive
summary.

**Proposition 2: a cached positive history remains a modeled witness.**
Induct on the length of an exact prefix. The initial summary starts at f0.
For an inductive extension, each retained frame has a valid history for that
same prefix, and a relation summary supplies a legal local step from that exact
frame. Appending the step yields a continuous valid history. Deduplicating equal
whole frames keeps a valid representative without creating combinations of
bindings. Known-contract state excludes indeterminate invariant history from
a supported witness. Independent certificate replay supplies an additional
check of the emitted concrete modeled trace.

**Proposition 3: exact-prefix exhaustion supports only the declared negative.**
If an entire prefix has no legal known lift and no indeterminate assignment or
contract state, no extension of that prefix can be a legal safe completing
modeled trace. Recording that exact prefix is therefore sound. If bounded
enumeration and the certificate checker cover every relevant abstract goal
path, contract observations are determinate and both model and relation
coverage are complete, all completing modeled traces are excluded. Removing
any completeness or knownness assumption invalidates the unsupported conclusion
and yields unknown under the implementation's conservative rules.

**Proposition 4: cache reuse does not establish a performance bound.**
Relation reuse can reduce repeated expansions to the number of distinct
edge/before-frame pairs encountered, but exact abstract prefixes and their
histories need not be polynomially bounded in the horizon. With branching b,
the number of bounded paths can grow as a geometric sum through depth H.
The unchanged negative certificate may still enumerate exponentially many
refutations, and its checker repeats lifting without the solver cache.
Consequently fewer relation checks alone imply neither fewer total declared
units nor lower solver-plus-checker time. The stress result in Section 7
directly illustrates this distinction.

These propositions do not imply identical finite-budget decisions between
original and shared refinement: shared cache accounting consumes additional
allowance. They do not strengthen existential completion to an adversarial
winning policy. A deployment also needs fresh evidence, source-faithful legal
actions and attribution of the resulting application effects.

## 6. Experimental design

### 6.1. Development corpus and arms

The fixed scaling suite contains 66 related authored profiles in five families:
argument products (16), layered reconvergence (16), correlated frames (16),
horizon boundaries (6) and off-goal load (12). Family members deliberately vary
finite branching, relation acceptance, binding context, evidence/coverage or
horizon. They are development variants rather than independent held-out tasks,
representative protocol failures or human-validated application gold.

Four methods receive identical profile bytes, suite order, legal recipes,
relations, initial frames, contract, horizon and evaluation clock 100:
legacy relational direct, goal-pruned memoized direct, original refinement and
shared lifting. Each is evaluated at allowances 32, 256, 4,096 and 100,000.
The comparison captures selected solver and checker modules and profile bytes
prospectively; executed evidence is preserved in exclusive versioned allocations.
The separate fifth-method experiment uses those exact profiles, clock and
horizon, with the original legal recipe interpreter and negative checker.
Its fresh legacy baseline matches all 264 preserved baseline outputs. This
supplement was declared after the original study and is reported as such;
it is not a fifth arm retroactively inserted into the original allocation.

### 6.2. Outcome and resource accounting

Certified coverage counts supported or unsupported outputs with valid result
shape and independently accepted certificates. Every table cell uses all 66
planned profiles as its denominator. Unknown, execution error, timeout and
checker rejection cannot be dropped or reclassified as a certified decision.
The retained complete comparison contains 1,056 algorithm/profile/allowance
rows, 2,003 worker invocations and 683 accepted algorithm certificate checks.
There are 373 unknown algorithm rows. No worker error, timeout, rejected
certificate or input/source mutation was recorded in that matrix. The separate
Linux supplement contributes 264 comparator cells and 88 unknowns. The combined
analysis therefore retains 1,320 cells and 461 unknowns. Its 1,140 worker
dispatches include a fresh baseline and references, not 1,140 comparator tasks.
A prior Windows supplemental attempt timed out in a reference worker; that
failed allocation remains retained and is not included as a successful matrix.

Solver work totals include all 66 attempts, including unknowns. Certificate size
is the UTF-8 byte count of sorted compact JSON with non-ASCII characters retained
and nonfinite values rejected. Unknown proof size is missing, not a zero-byte
proof. Each certificate worker has allowance 2,000,000 and a 30-second timeout;
the frozen checkers return a boolean, so their internal work counters are not
available as measured costs.

Timing separates solver evaluation, checker execution and worker-wall
observations. Worker-wall measurements include startup, imports and output;
overlapping fields are not summed. Sequential process memory peaks are not
added to create an application memory total. Windows PeakWorkingSetSize and
Linux ru_maxrss are different operating-system observations. A single fixed-order
run on either host cannot establish a speed or memory ranking.

### 6.3. Paired analysis and reproducibility

The primary comparison pairs shared-lifting and goal-pruned-direct certification
on each identical profile and allowance. We report both-certified, direct-only,
shared-only and neither-certified counts. The paired coverage difference is
`(shared-only - direct-only) / 66`.

A supplementary analysis explores dependence on the composition of the five
existing development families. It draws five complete families with replacement
per replicate, uses their full profile totals as weights, and takes the
inclusive 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles over 2,000 replicates with seed 1729. These
ranges describe sensitivity to resampling the authored families. They are not
population confidence intervals, do not supply independent observations and
are not used to claim statistical significance or generalization.

The independent analysis reconstructs 11,640 table cells without importing the
analysis generator, recomputes canonical proof sizes and verifies the input and
output bindings. A separate standard Ubuntu reproduction compares deterministic
solver, reference and checker projections against the retained Windows results.
Only explicitly identified runtime observations are excluded from deterministic
equality. Independent auditing uses trusted local checkers on downloaded
evidence rather than executing downloaded source.

## 9. Discussion

The [method contribution assessment](../docs/research/method-contribution-assessment-v2.md)
and [independent static code peer](../docs/research/method-contribution-code-peer-v1.json)
support an executable finite admission/certificate and empirical artifact,
while leaving a distinct general method and qualified full study unresolved.
The direct comparator already supplies whole-frame relation caching,
remaining-horizon goal pruning and first-feasible goal return. Shared lifting
adds exact-prefix/current-history reuse and complete relation summaries while
retaining path-prefix refutation certificates. These implemented obligations
do not alone establish a new CEGAR, memoization or constraint-pruning principle.

The main negative result is not merely that one cache incurred overhead.
It identifies a separation between the unit of reuse and the unit of proof.
The relation cache reuses an edge evaluated from one complete frame, while the
negative certificate still names path-prefix refutations. In reconvergence,
many abstract paths can share a small relation product yet require a large
explicit prefix cover in the retained representation. Reduced relation
evaluations consequently fail to compress the proof or eliminate history and
indexing charges. The direct product comparator exposes this representation
choice rather than letting an inefficient historical baseline hide it.

This result constrains, but does not settle, future method design. A compressed
proof or structurally shared certificate would require an independently
reconstructive checker and new validation obligations. Reusing cached decisions
across calls would additionally require a sound invalidation basis tied to
profile, caller context, evidence and state change. None of those mechanisms is
measured here. They cannot be credited to the current implementation merely
because their benefits are plausible.

The benchmark's evidence architecture is useful independently of the adverse
method result. It preserves the difference between unknown and a checked
negative, binds solver and checker dispatches, keeps all planned cases in the
denominator and records application effects separately. The Linux reproduction
shows that the deterministic claims survive a second operating-system
execution. The original-application fixture shows why checking public
task/context continuity and attributed effects matters after a positive model
answer. These are demonstrated artifact properties; their novelty and broader
research significance require comparison and author judgment.

Finally, a research completion score cannot replace validity. A full draft,
more authored cases or additional synthetic tests cannot supply missing
independent human domain review, genuine provider execution or evidence of a
distinct contribution. The present manuscript reports the established result
without turning those absent lanes into successes.

## 10. Threats to validity and limitations

**Model fidelity.** Source references and coverage declarations are input
assumptions. Complete finite-profile negatives do not prove complete real-route
negatives. Arbitrary string inputs, changing task status, alternative public
reads, principals, approval availability and transport configuration need
source-faithful coverage or an explicit restricted claim.

**Development dependence.** The profiles are related authored variants selected
to exercise particular mechanisms. They are neither independent application
templates nor sampled deployments. Family resampling does not repair that
limitation. The stress case motivated caching and is disclosed as such.

Shared lifting explicitly charges canonical cache-key, payload and history
traversal; the direct language's key serialization and domain-key construction
do not carry comparable meter charges. Equal numerical allowances therefore
compare declared method-specific accounting policies, not equal CPU or hardware
budgets. The adverse coverage result cannot by itself establish practical
efficiency or a general speed ranking.

**Comparator and meter scope.** Goal-pruned direct is a stronger comparator
than the historical direct implementation, but the study is not a comparison
against all established symbolic or contract tools. Work meters charge
different operations. Internal checker work is unavailable, and single-order
wall time and operating-system peak memory do not support general performance
rankings.

**Software and proof assurance.** Focused tests and independent certificate
workers exercise meaningful tampering, correlation, unknown, horizon, freshness
and interruption boundaries. They are not a formal proof of implementation
correctness. Independently authored AI checker/audit logic remains assistance
within the same research workflow and does not replace independent human
scientific review. Retained hashes establish byte consistency, not capture
authenticity or universal runtime provenance.

**Application external validity.** The original AgentOps effect result uses
one deterministic confirmation fixture and does not execute shared lifting.
The subsequent 34-candidate governed-App census and original ADK shopper
allocation add source-bound offline feasibility observations; their incomplete
recipes and unrealized branches remain distinct from qualified task outcomes. Full AP2 shopper qualification, both-bridge transfer, human-validated held-out gold,
mixed-model behavior and live conversational utility remain unqualified.

**Literature and authorship.** Citation versions and provenance are retained and
structurally checked. Twenty-two identities were refreshed against primary
sources for this version, with retrieval failures and metadata gaps retained.
No supervisor novelty approval, independent human task/gold review or submission
approval is inferred from AI drafting or a supplied pilot-number review. The
author remains responsible for checking the argument, references and eventual
publication claims.

## 12. Conclusion

Bounded task-relative admission can distinguish a checked public completion,
a complete finite-model obstruction and unresolved evidence, while an
application-level evaluation separately verifies whether the original task and
required effect actually complete. The implemented shared-lifting adaptation
preserves those boundaries but shows no certified-coverage advantage over
goal-pruned direct search in the four-allowance development study. Cache reuse
reduces repeated relation checks on reconvergence while the uncompressed proof
and charged bookkeeping remain expensive. The deterministic result reproduces
on Linux, and an original-refinement admitted AgentOps fixture reaches the
expected effect with clean lifecycle evidence. An explicitly intervened original
AP2 sequence resumes both tasks to a corresponding demo Success receipt.
A subsequent shared-lifting-gated AP2 development sequence also reaches
a separately observed demo receipt after independently checked pre-send admission.
The separate fifth comparator reinforces the adverse aggregate result.
These are concrete, verified
research results at their stated scope. Algorithmic novelty, held-out
two-domain utility and live-model interoperability remain open scientific
claims.
