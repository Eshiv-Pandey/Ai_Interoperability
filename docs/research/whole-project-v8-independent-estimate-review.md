profiles with five certified same-decision paired repeats. There are no failed
or timed-out cells, certified contradictions, hash-seed mismatches or
exhaustion-label discrepancies in this run. The 300 unknown cells remain in
the full denominator and outside cost ratios.

This run supplies measured full-worker CPU, wall time and peak RSS alongside
the earlier method-specific work counters. CPU and wall caps are common;
`RLIMIT_AS` is correctly described as an address-space cap, with RSS observed
without a separate enforced RSS-limit claim. Costs include fresh interpreter,

it adds no certified profiles and its paired median CPU, wall and RSS costs
exceed the goal-pruned and guard-pruned direct comparators on all 54 eligible
profiles. This bounded
completed comparison supports measurement 70 to 75 and experiments 55 to 60,
adding two further weighted points. It does not establish universal speed,
novelty or live downstream utility.
