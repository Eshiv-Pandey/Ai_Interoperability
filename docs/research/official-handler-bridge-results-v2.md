# Official-handler bridge fidelity results v2

24 October 2026. **Four once-only bridge cases completed; the tested logical
completion goal failed in all four.** Native header-positive controls completed
the original task/context in each isolated service. B1-B3 typed answers reached
the official handler but omitted references and left the task input-required.
B4's raw answer and raw final GetTask were rejected for the absent version header.
All four evaluator-only final reads found the original task/context still pending.

These are bounded controls with the unchanged disclosed authored Carol executor,
an authored 1.0-only card and the genuine SDK1.1.0 Express handler. They do not
establish natural failure prevalence, a general protocol defect, a novel method,
a qualifying second application domain or human scientific review. CP2 remains
in progress and verified deliverable credit remains **5%**.

deviation; B3/B4 also hit the nullable-notification cost bug. B1's initial replay
attempted 25 mutations but three selectors failed during setup. Those failures
were never counted as rejections. All four original replay archives were unchanged
and their complete replay claims failed.

The separate [post-capture correction v2.1](official-handler-bridge-checker-correction-v2.1.md)

pending expectation with a stronger, source-valid pending witness inside the
existing public pre-answer GetTask and unchanged 5s bound. SUBMITTED observations
and original deviations remain preserved. This is a corrected software check
of the same bytes, not a retroactively pre-registered prediction or service rerun.

All four corrected checks and isolated replays pass. Actual stdin requested/written
bytes and stdout/stderr read/forward pairs match. Complete mgcrea request and
ordered response/notification/unparsed streams reconstruct from the real pipes;
the caller diagnostics match. Actual PIDs, launch/configuration, source Git receipts,
PID/run-bound shutdown files, pipe EOFs and terminal exits match their sidecars.
Source/runtime hashes remained unchanged. The replay imports no SDK/driver and
performs zero service requests. Original and corrected checker/replay versions
are archived together.
