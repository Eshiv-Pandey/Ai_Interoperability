## Actual development findings

Reference, placebo annotation and harmless record reordering each complete
10/10 constructed tasks. Removing records, changing tool binding, changing
task/target or removing constraints each completes 0/10. A separate authorized
snapshot-reread policy completes 10/10 after the same constructed missing
records. Initial conversion loss remains counted after recovery.

These are expected controlled-software outcomes. They validate the distinction
between fact groups, usable operations and task outcome; they are not measured
LLM performance or a newly discovered real bridge failure rate. Historical
observations are separately retained, including deliberate native negatives.

## Scientific limits

New tasks and expected outcomes are author/AI-authored development material.
Human review and untouched evaluation remain pending. Context scores count
declared fact groups, not total information content. A failed observed attempt
does not prove route infeasibility. Native versus bridge costs are not compared
across unmatched capture scopes. No novelty, generality, publication-readiness
or original full-study completion claim is made.
