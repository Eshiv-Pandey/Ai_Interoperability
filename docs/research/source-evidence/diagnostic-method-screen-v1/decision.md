## Exact restricted question

Keep the supplied finite continuation contract `(s0, I, G, H)` and its accepted
evidence boundary. Separately supply a finite family of source-justified edit
choices `e`, legality constraints `K(e)`, an edited transition relation `R_e`,
and a nonnegative edit cost `c(e)`. The base route is `R_0`. Edits belong to an
explicit diagnostic/design problem; they are not tools newly granted to the
receiving caller. Source modifications, changed service profiles and outside
authorization cannot become recovery actions in the original study by renaming
them repairs.

The restricted diagnostic predicate is

`Repair(e) := K(e) and exists h <= H, s1,...,sh:
  all visited states satisfy I, all steps belong to R_e, and G(sh).`

Find a legal `e` minimizing `c(e)` with a safe completion witness. A baseline
enumerates the finite edits and invokes exact bounded reachability. A symbolic
baseline instead unrolls the same finite relations and constraints, jointly
solves for `e` and the trace, and minimizes the supplied cost. To cover length
zero through H, use a disjunction of length-h formulas; unconstrained padding
must not invent a permitted stutter action. Non-additive costs require an exact
encoding or explicit enumeration, rather than claiming every cost is MaxSAT.

For this supplied finite, fully known fragment, each legal edited safe trace
satisfies its corresponding formula, and each satisfying assignment decodes to
such a trace. This elementary encoding equivalence is structural baseline
reasoning, not a new theorem or executed solver result. Partial evidence or
indeterminate predicates preserve unknowns. A global no-repair certificate also
requires completeness of the declared edit family, legal changes and relevant
route model. Neither four failures nor one attempted edit establishes it.

Subset-minimal, minimum-cost and a single minimizing explanation are different
outputs. For a claimed minimum, checking its positive trace is insufficient:
all cheaper legal edits must be refuted under the accepted finite model. All
co-minimal alternatives require an enumeration/coverage check. A local unsat
core is relative to its formula and assumptions; it is not automatically the
unique defective component or a full-interface obstruction.
