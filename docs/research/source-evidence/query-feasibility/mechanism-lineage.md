| [Ciliberto, Rudi and Tassi, PC2 v1, 20 October 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28484v1) | Section 3.1: "querying external services, or combining several sources". Section 3.3 / Definition 4 and Section 3.4 / Algorithm 1; Lemma 1: "not the elapsed time". | A view may combine observations. | [Golovin, Krause and Ray, NIPS 2010, EC2](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2010/file/1e6e0a04d20f50967c64dac2d639a577-Paper.pdf) | Section 2, PDF page 2: "also called a conditional plan". Neither exact-hypothesis identification nor independence of all tests is a mandatory comparator restriction. No Bayesian guarantee transfers to v0 minimax costs without assumptions/proof. |
| [Javdani et al., AISTATS 2014, HEC](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.pdf) | Section 2, pp.431-432: "the decision regions to overlap"; footnote 2 supports nonuniform costs. A valid stopping policy puts every consistent hypothesis in one successful decision region; multiple regions may overlap. Sections 3.3-3.4 provide expected-cost guarantees using adaptive submodularity/monotonicity. Alternative valid path certificates naturally define overlapping regions. Expiry is an adaptation to the paper's assumptions, not a guarantee inherited from it. |
| [Bonet and Geffner, AAAI 2012](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/8365/8224) | "Contingent Planning", p.1757: "all possible executions end up in a goal belief". Offline policies cover all possible executions; online policies act from the current belief. V0's finite receipt/time/budget state and min-max recursion are a direct finite AND/OR specialization, a structural derivation rather than an assertion that this paper gives its exact syntax. |

The source review supports a narrow comparison, not a complete novelty search. A different completion quantifier or a renamed task field alone does not establish that advance.
