# Partial-order reduction comparator screen

25 October 2026. Root analytical screen; no independent or human assessment,
new algorithm, implementation or performance result.

The inspected primary version is Baelde, Delaune and Hirschi,
[POR for Security Protocol Equivalences: Beyond Action-Determinism, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.03650v2).
The HTML conversion exposed no usable paper body. The PDF passages are
retained in pairs 05/06/08-11. The parity-game DOI encountered a nonretryable
access error; discovery abstract information is not a full method audit.

Definition 3 and Propositions 1/2 in Section 3 give the concrete
enabledness/commutation and final-state reachability basis for persistent
and sleep sets. Section 4's paired configurations and ghosts retain
non-equivalence witnesses. Section 5 does more than inspect disjoint
syntactic action names: Definitions 7/8 cover both enabled and disabled
actions, including observations that enable new input recipes.
Proposition 5 establishes concrete independence under symbolic conditions.
Definition 9 and Proposition 6 connect symbolic stubborn sets to concrete
persistent sets. Proposition 7 supplies the symbolic sleep-execution
completeness direction. Section 5.5/Proposition 8 justifies computing those
dependencies after a conservative conditional transformation. Section 6
connects this reduction to final bad-state witnesses and reports integration
with Apte/DeepSec. These are the actual numbered results; the nonmatching
Theorem finds do not indicate absent correctness results.

[Pair 10](10-transaction-boundaries-and-por-tool-response.json) contains
Propositions 5/6/8 and Section 6; [pair 11](11-por-completeness-and-application-factory-tool-response.json)
contains Proposition 7 and the history-dependent persistent assignment.
The existing [action/observation screen](../action-observation-closure-screen-v1/decision.md)
already requires correlated observations and legal recipe coverage.
Generic symbolic POR, dependency fixed points, memoization, conditional
collapse or checking a task monitor in a product state is consequently an
established comparator direction, not an identified extra C1 step.

Transferring this comparator to the approved admission property has specific
proof obligations. Equivalence-witness preservation alone does not prove
bounded safe original-task may-completion, cheapest completion, or a uniform
winning caller policy. An application monitor must retain original task/
context, allowed principal/effect/audit conditions, caller observations,
legal input construction, expiry and consumed horizon. Independence must
be sound on that augmented semantics. For example, reading a confirmation
handle can enable new legal confirm recipes even when method/resource names
look different. Two local writes may commute on separate service rows yet
differ in the audited ordering, task monitor, approval availability or
elapsed deadline. Removing one ordering from those superficial conditions
would require a separate proof; it is not justified by the inspected paper.

An ordinary monitor/product extension and conservative dependency analysis
are baseline work. Any proposed additional step must name a concrete
source transformation or representation, exhibit a distinction against
optimized symbolic/POR and contract comparators on a common fragment, and
prespecify an advantage before a trial. Cost must include source/schema/
transport extraction, dependency/stubborn-set exploration, constraint
solving, monitor construction, certificate checking and caching/setup,
as well as any public probes. Exact baseline correctness on identical
complete finite evidence cannot be beaten by inventing a different target
or excluding legal lucky string guesses.

No candidate satisfying that distinction is selected. The narrow decision
is adverse for generic POR novelty; it does not establish that no novel
method is possible. No performance threshold, corpus freeze, experiment,
theorem for the interoperability property, human review or credit is added.
