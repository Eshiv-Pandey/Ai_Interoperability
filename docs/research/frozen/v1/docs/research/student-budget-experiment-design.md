## Scope and novelty gate

Study task-relative **finite may-compatibility**: whether at least one permitted continuation exists through a pinned route within the declared horizon. Return supported with a checked constructive/sufficient certificate, unsupported with a valid bounded witness, or unknown when evidence/model completeness is insufficient. Keep modeled safety and realized LLM compliance separate; no universal progress or opaque-service guarantee is planned.

The method must improve on correctly modeled composition, not only schema/feature checks. [Interface Automata](https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/record/4622), [predicate transformers](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/transcriptions/EWD04xx/EWD472.html), [contract quotient](https://eskang.github.io/assets/papers/memocode18.pdf), [IR-MIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09035) and [Pacti](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17751) already cover substantial compatibility/residual reasoning. Dijkstra's total-correctness `wp` is stronger than existential reachability. Also compare AgentThread, CONTINUITY, BRIDGE and AgentAsk using the main workflow's audited sources. Adding task IDs, principals, expiry or three decision labels alone is not algorithmic novelty.

Require a first-week concrete distinction: checking/evidence cost, reusable certified analysis, compact independently checkable certificates or useful coverage under a stated checking budget. If the procedure merely reproduces standard predecessor propagation, withdraw that algorithmic-novelty claim before scaling.

Remaining edges use reference delivery. Check that the actual bridges support the composed placements; do not invent an inverse conversion to fill the matrix. This scripted topology is separate from the two-role, one-handoff live validation.

Distinguish expected non-support, disclosed degradation, unexpected implementation failure, environment failure and injected faults. Keep same-runtime-evidence comparators: schema/features, contract linting/per-edge checks, exact bounded product may-reachability, credible symbolic/backward and cached/incremental versions for cost claims, and applicable contract/quotient methods on common fragments. They get the same contract, profiles, evidence and horizon as the gate; private gold is reserved for the independently implemented grader. Exact complete reachability cannot lose correctness within its accepted model; any advantage must concern cost, evidence, certificates or budgeted coverage.

## Analysis, uncertainty and reproduction

Metric continuity follows the main workflow's explicit mapping. Task success supplies the primary live contrast, not the sole retained measure. Context/capability preservation, constraints/tool fidelity, recovery, latency/messages/bytes and token/call overhead remain planned diagnostic or secondary areas. Admission validity, unknowns, coverage, certificates and checking/evidence-query cost are primary core method outcomes. The original six acronyms lack complete source definitions; reconcile formulas and denominators before freezing rather than claiming five were deleted or all six remain unchanged. Graders and trace collectors still require implementation and validation.

Strict success requires all original goals with no forbidden intermediate/final actions. Unknown, unsupported and refusal are not completion. Report all tasks, oracle-eligible/ineligible strata, paired wins/losses/ties, violations, coverage, witness validity, extraction misses and actual cost/latency. Correct rejection can still reduce utility. Native/conventional contrasts and direction breakdowns are secondary; there is one primary live contrast.

Average trials within template; retain all nested arms/trials together in a template bootstrap and use higher-family sensitivity when needed. Domain/direction are strata, not independent clusters. With only 12/24 independent templates, uncertainty is exploratory and wide: hypothetical paired template-mean variance 0.09 gives approximate 95% half-widths ±17/±12 points. This cannot promise small-effect detection, model rankings or broad vendor generalization.

Freeze sample, thresholds, prompts, extraction, policies, source interfaces, horizon, scorer, exclusions/retries, diagnostics and analysis before confirmation. Retain nulls, gains, failures and incomplete outputs. Model/scorer corrections create a new version; do not selectively overwrite failures. Quota stops follow predeclared handling rather than favorable stopping.

Independent reproduction: replay the complete scripted suite and all headline evidence; regenerate full analysis from retained records. Fresh live subset starts at four templates balanced over domain/direction × two trials × informed-recovery/proposed arms: 8 new prefixes and 16 continuations, or 4 prefixes/8 receivers per model. At s=2k/r=4k this is 40k/model, 48k with margin, before follow-ups; it fits 85k. It is a subset rerun, not a fresh repetition of the full study or additional independent templates.

## Forty-five-day execution gates

Assume two students at 10–15 focused hours/week: roughly 130–190 person-hours, plus real peer/supervisor review. One owns the gate/runtime; the other independently derives oracle/gold. Measure template-review and integration time; 68 authored development/calibration/held-out templates are a real workload, not free generated diversity.

| Days | Required exit |
| --- | --- |
| 1–7 | Tiny real paths/oracle; substantive novelty distinction against whole-route compatibility |
| 8–14 | Two domains, pinned bridges, strong local comparators, validated reset/grading |
| 15–21 | Human review; bounded pilot; actual quota ledger and conservative prospective N |
| 22–28 | Sealed tasks/subsets; immutable method/settings/analysis; begin local confirmation |
| 29–35 | Fixed local and live evidence, failures retained; optional Opus only if arranged |
| 36–42 | Independent complete local replay, small live rerun, wide uncertainty and adverse review |

Deliver pinned code/configurations, independent oracle, task/gold and human-review records, per-model ledger, raw traces, strong comparator/ablation evidence, reproducible figures and a complete manuscript. A systems/protocol contribution is the primary positioning; a large NLP/vendor benchmark is outside this budget.
