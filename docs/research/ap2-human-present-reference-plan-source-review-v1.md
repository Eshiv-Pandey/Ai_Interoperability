## Disclosed corrections and limitations

Before capture, source review requested and the owner fixed dynamic CP card URL,
typed approval equality, boolean checkout amount, ContactAddress field shape and
malformed Part ambiguity. The final reread confirms those changes. Root's
transactional-rejection concern is also closed by the local-copy/final-commit
structure; the reviewer made no helper/CLI/test edits.

The supplied message nonce is checked only as lowercase 32-hex shape. It is
explicitly labelled a caller-supplied planning nonce, not an original builder
execution. It proves neither UUID4 version/variant bits, randomness, freshness,
uniqueness nor caller control over the original builder-generated message ID.
The source-exact message label refers to the reviewed text/data/context/task
form subject to that supplied planning placeholder; it is not actual wire or
generation provenance.

The planner rejects repeated raw task IDs among newly initiated supplied
prelude phases. This is a stricter reference-plan consistency condition, not a
proof that independently namespaced service task IDs are globally unique or
that the original UUID generator cannot collide. Native identity must later be
bound to service/endpoint and original request/response association. The
planner's supported text/data Part subset is also narrower than complete A2A
Part/schema support. These restrictions must not become whole-interface
non-support or source-authenticated admission claims.

Supplied signer strings and CP public completion do not establish valid
mandates, an actual original signer execution or token-to-transaction business
association. Supplied checkout hash agreement does not verify its merchant
signature or authority. Public OTP availability in this scripted merchant
boundary does not repair or qualify the separate original ADK shopper's
status-only challenge projection. The original merchant router/history
heuristic stays unchanged and unexecuted.

The 24 methods are inspected authored software checks, not tests executed by
this reviewer. The preserved owner capture and any subsequent independent
receipt audit must substantiate execution/membership/preservation separately.
No partial planner or refusal CLI may be represented as a complete native
runner. Fresh isolated sources/dependencies, original loaded-byte binding,
multi-service ownership/recording, actual-summary authorization, timed resource
gate, immediate bootstrap validation, prospective usage accounting, native
original task/effect qualification and human review remain required.
