# Publication strategy for the 45-day research sprint

Calls checked on 2026-10-23; execution calendar aligned with approval on 2026-10-24. Research sprint: **24 October through 7 December 2026, inclusive**. This is a local planning document; it is not a submission or a paper draft.

The realistic Day 45 objective is an independently checked research result, executable artifact and complete manuscript for author review. Conference acceptance, revision, camera-ready preparation and proceedings publication occur later. The current [workflow](../../RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md) treats all models and quotas as unconfirmed; 1M/model is only a planning illustration. Local protocol/method evidence is primary; any live sample is chosen from actual verified resources before confirmation, and claims follow that coverage.

## Recommended publication route

**Choose the venue by the contribution that survives the novelty gate. ACL 2027 through its January 2027 ARR cycle is a previously source-verified feasible deadline, conditional on an actual contribution to LLM-agent semantics and evaluation.** The audited main-conference deadline is **4 January 2027, 23:59 AoE**, to be rechecked before submission. That gives 28 calendar days after the Day 45 milestone for independent review, corrections and preparation. The inspected CFP includes LLM Agents for NLP and accepts resources, negative findings and reproducibility research. It automatically considers submissions not selected for the main conference for Findings. Its commitment, notification and camera-ready dates remained TBA in that audit; conference sessions were listed as 20-22 August 2027. [Official ACL 2027 CFP](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/), [official calendar](https://2027.aclweb.org/).

The candidate contribution is **task-relative admission or refusal of required continuation operations across composed, actual MCP/A2A mappings**. Under declared capability, handle, scope, timing and service assumptions, a practical gate would return supported, unsupported or unknown with a checkable witness, and choose a permitted compatible route or request an explicit alternative. An independent finite-continuation oracle would evaluate the gate; held-out local tasks, independent mappings and a small live study would test usefulness. This is proposed positioning, not an achieved or proven-novel result. Generic structured handoffs, contract preservation, bounded composition checking and verified repair already have close precedents. The [novelty review](six-week-novelty-review.md) and [current student-budget experiment design](student-budget-experiment-design.md) define the current controls; the larger [six-week design](six-week-experiment-design.md) is historical.

**Retain AAMAS as the strongest specialist audience and NeurIPS evaluation as the strongest benchmark-oriented audience.** AAMAS 2027's Generative and Agentic AI and Engineering Multiagent Systems areas explicitly cover interaction protocols, interoperability, testing and evaluation, but its main-track deadline has passed. NeurIPS renamed Datasets & Benchmarks to Evaluations & Datasets in 2026; that scope includes evaluation methodology and audits without requiring a new model. Its 2026 deadline has also passed. Use these venues' requirements to strengthen the work; decide later whether a future edition is preferable to the known ACL opportunity. [AAMAS 2027 main CFP](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/calls/call-for-main-track/), [NeurIPS 2026 evaluation CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets).

If the primary finding is instead about protocol correctness or software testing, retain that identity and target a suitable systems/software venue. Do not force an NLP story just to meet a date. A large transport-comparison table without a new scientific insight is weak positioning for either direction.

This is particularly relevant to the student revision: a strong bounded operation-admission result can remain defensible with a small LLM diagnostic, while a broad NLP benchmark claim would require more evidence. Astra/Sol establish one-family mixed-model validation; Opus, if arranged, supports only a limited two-family check. Choose paper claims and venue after the novelty and evidence gates, not from the earlier subjective scores.

## Evidence reviewers should be able to inspect

ARR explicitly distinguishes scientific soundness from the interest of a contribution. AAMAS additionally lists originality, significance, reproducibility and appropriate treatment of prior work. The following is our evidence plan, not an official numeric scoring rubric. [ARR review form](https://aclrollingreview.org/reviewform), [AAMAS criteria](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/calls/call-for-main-track/).

| Reviewer concern | Evidence required before recommending submission | What prevents a submission recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Is this original beyond AgentThread, CONTINUITY and BRIDGE?” | Versioned claim-to-source ledger and property-level comparisons against these closest works, AgentAsk, PACT and classical compatibility/quotient methods; a demonstrated advantage over same-runtime-evidence bounded reachability and relevant contract analysis. | First bounded verification despite AgentThread; generic runtime admission despite CONTINUITY; capability-aware repair despite BRIDGE or rerouting despite AgentAsk; standard backward propagation relabeled as a new algorithm; novelty based only on combining familiar axes. |
| “Does the experiment isolate interoperability?” | Matched direct and delegated controls; frozen upstream artifacts replayed across independently implemented mappings; same prompts, evidence, tools and budgets; explicit separation of payload loss, unavailable continuation operations, lifecycle and model effects. | Changing sender model, receiver model, prompt length and protocol together; deliberately broken fields presented as natural protocol failures; assuming MCP and A2A offer equivalent operations. |
| “Do the admission decisions and outcomes measure what they claim?” | Independently implemented finite reachability oracle; supported/unsupported/unknown confusion table, accepted-route false acceptance, eligible-route false rejection, coverage and witness checks; deterministic state/event outcomes; positive/negative controls and grader mutation tests; declared finite horizon and assumptions. | Calling unknown a correct rejection, injecting evaluator gold into the practical gate, an unvalidated judge treated as an oracle, or a bounded result presented as universal correctness. |
| “Does the method improve practical continuation?” | Two-domain local core, independent oracle and same-evidence strong comparators; component ablations; a 12/24-template one-family live diagnostic with paired uncertainty and measured extraction limits; Opus supplementary if arranged; independent bridge transfer only when its gate passes; completion, violations, coverage and overhead together. | Privileged evidence, hidden calls, confirmation tuning, a small diagnostic called broad vendor evidence, manually specified contracts presented as reliable extraction, or refusing nearly all useful work without reporting lost coverage. |
| “Are differences reliable?” | Prespecified primary outcomes and comparisons; domain-stratified paired template bootstrap intervals, retaining related instances/arms/trials together and checking higher shared decision-family dependence; disclosed exclusions, failures and multiplicity handling; practically meaningful effect sizes. | Treating instances or repeated runs from one authored template as independent scenarios, selective reporting or a significance claim without uncertainty. |
| “Can another group reproduce this?” | Pinned protocol/SDK versions and mapping provenance; model IDs and run dates; task seeds, contracts, state/event traces and minimized witnesses; one-command local replay; trace-derived tables; sealed analysis; independent rerun of a representative subset and independent sign-off of headline claims. | Missing traces, grading criteria withheld from reviewers, mutable model aliases without provenance, irreproducible external environments or contributors signing off their own headline findings. |
| “Is it useful outside synthetic toys?” | A small independently constructed application-style task set and at least one external-adapter validation; limits on generalization stated explicitly. | Universal enterprise claims drawn entirely from one internally generated task grammar. |

More paid runs cannot compensate for any of these missing controls. A null or negative finding can be valuable when the isolation design and implications are strong.

| Days 43-45, 5-7 December | Complete the local paper, artifact, related-work refresh and claim-to-evidence audit for author/supervisor review. Decide whether the study is ready, needs narrower claims, or should be delayed; record that decision in a publication-readiness report. |

Local execution starts on 24 October after workflow approval. The calendar preserves 45 days rather than compressing validation to preserve a venue deadline. Writing starts in week one and proceeds with the research. ## Human ownership, AI use and submission logistics

The named student researchers and supervisor must understand and defend the research questions, design, sources, data and conclusions. Record substantive human contributions from the beginning. No AI system is an author. Under current ACL ethics, human authors must approve the published version and accept accountability; content-producing AI assistance must be disclosed appropriately. Literature suggestions must be checked by reading the sources. Record assistance in research/code/analysis now so later disclosure is accurate. These are current ACL requirements, not an assertion that every venue uses identical rules. [ACL publication ethics](https://www.aclweb.org/adminwiki/index.php/ACL_Policy_on_Publication_Ethics).

Starting with October 2026 ARR, a qualified designated service contributor is needed to guarantee reviewing; otherwise submissions enter the stated capacity lottery. All authors need accurate OpenReview profiles and ORCID. Confirm whether an actual team member meets the qualification rules; do not add a nominal author to satisfy this requirement. Complete the service-contributor registration and Responsible NLP Checklist within the applicable deadlines. Recheck the January instructions before submission. [ARR sustainable reviewing policy](https://aclrollingreview.org/sustainable-reviewing-2026), [ARR dates](https://aclrollingreview.org/dates).

Prepare an anonymous reviewer copy of code/data with neutral URLs, scrubbed author metadata and no author-revealing history. Preserve a separately maintained full provenance record. Review access should not require contacting us. Evaluator-private gold is withheld from runtime arms during confirmation; the validated grading criteria, reference specifications and appropriate sealed task records must be reviewer-accessible afterward. The NeurIPS 2026 evaluation track requires executable benchmark artifacts at submission and structured dataset metadata; use that as a useful readiness standard while rechecking the eventual venue's actual requirements. [NeurIPS artifact policy](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets).

## Archival publication and release decisions

ACL main and Findings are archival publication routes; a preprint is public dissemination without conference acceptance. A workshop may be archival or nonarchival: verify its actual proceedings and duplicate-submission rules before choosing it. Do not treat a workshop presentation as a main-conference publication. AAMAS main/Findings and NeurIPS evaluation tracks explicitly publish proceedings. [ACL CFP](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/), [AAMAS CFP](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/calls/call-for-main-track/), [NeurIPS CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets).

Do not submit substantially overlapping work concurrently to archival venues. A nonarchival workshop can sometimes support feedback before an archival submission, but only when both venues' policies allow it. ICML's current policy explicitly distinguishes workshops without published proceedings; that is a useful example rather than a 2027 rule guarantee. [ICML 2026 policy](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers).

Publish a preprint or repository only after the authors approve the evidence, claims, disclosure and venue compatibility. An early public timestamp does not prove novelty or scientific correctness. Registration, proceedings charges where applicable and author presentation commitments should be budgeted separately from experiment APIs.

The strongest submission recommendation is: the contribution remains distinct under close prior-art comparison; the central claim is supported by controls, honest uncertainty and an independently executable artifact; and human authors can defend every claimed result. If these conditions are unmet on Day 45, narrowing or postponing the submission protects the research.
