## Actual method distinction and strongest comparator

Goal-pruned direct already searches the bounded state/depth/whole-frame product,
caches complete edge/whole-frame relation results, prunes outside the remaining
safe goal cone and returns on the first feasible goal tuple. Its independently
authored negative checker reconstructs product closure without the solver cache
or witness history. Shared lifting instead enumerates abstract goal paths and
reuses exact-prefix frontiers plus complete edge/whole-frame expansions while
reattaching local steps to the current history. The negative proof still covers
infeasible path prefixes. Guard-pruned direct additionally rejects only closed,
definitely false partial argument assignments.

These are different implementations and useful test obligations. They are not
evidence of a new memoization, CEGAR or constraint-consistency principle. The
[retained primary-source synthesis](final-literature-review-v1.md) supplies that
closest-mechanism comparison, with its targeted-search and Mackworth full-body
limitations preserved. No external comparator system was reproduced here.

The [DAG codec](refutation-prefix-dag-v2.md) adds exact finite suffix-language
sharing and ordered-prefix reconstruction. Its adapter expands the supplied
graph and delegates the restored negative certificate to the unchanged semantic
checker. That is concrete lossless packaging with bounded validation, rather
than a directly compressed semantic-checking algorithm. Expansion/replay can
remain large. Neither ordinary suffix sharing nor passing a certificate through
a different wrapper establishes novelty by itself.

## What the codec adds, and what the aggregate hides

The exact [26-case codec receipt](refutation-prefix-dag-comparison-v2.json)
records all unsupported retained shared-lifting certificates. The new focused
read checks original/direct canonical proof sizes and hashes against the raw
solver rows. DAG bytes and roundtrip outcomes are inherited from that bound
receipt; no codec or semantic check is rerun.

| Same negative-proof subset | Original prefix bytes | DAG wrapper bytes | Direct proof bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All 26 cases | 15,128 | 11,918 | 7,760 |
| Excluding `scale-0031` | 6,233 | 10,182 | 7,422 |
| Excluding both prefix-shrinking cases | 5,498 | 9,530 | 7,084 |

The headline 3,210-byte reduction is real but concentrated: only two DAG
wrappers shrink relative to their prefix proofs and 24 grow. Removing the
largest stress case reverses the aggregate reduction to a 3,949-byte increase.
These removals are disclosed post hoc sensitivity calculations, not revised
primary samples or new experiments.

Two DAG wrappers are smaller than the corresponding direct proofs:
`scale-0039` is 540 versus 569 bytes, and `scale-0047` is 596 versus 893 bytes.
Their old prefix proofs were already smaller still, at 259 and 327 bytes.
Consequently these two direct-relative size wins do not establish a new
codec-induced advantage. Direct is smaller on the remaining 24 cases and in
aggregate, while the largest repeated-prefix proof remains 1,736 versus
direct's 338 bytes after encoding.

For all 54 accepted shared proofs, substituting the retained DAG sizes for the
26 negatives yields an arithmetic 24,038 bytes, versus 27,248 before packaging
and 19,880 for direct. This is a transparent composition of retained sizes,
not a newly executed integrated codec arm. It supplies no faster checker,
smaller measured process memory, higher admission coverage or business utility.

## Concrete contribution and remaining discriminating work

The defensible current result is the separation between relation reuse, proof
representation and actual worker cost. It is backed by a credible memoized
direct comparator, lossless packaging with adverse cases retained, bounded
independent certificate interfaces, all-attempt accounting and reproducible
source/dispatch evidence. Those are artifact and measurement contributions.
Their research significance and originality require accountable scientific
judgment; correct hashing, separate checker dispatches and three decision
labels are not themselves an invented verification principle.
