# Six-week novelty stress test: task-relative continuation compatibility

Prepared 2026-10-23 for the proposed 45-day study, approximately October 23–December 6, 2026. This is preparatory research and a recommendation for workflow approval. No system has been implemented and no cited experiment has been independently reproduced in this review. Public primary papers, specifications, and author repositories were inspected; their results remain author-reported. The earlier eight-day deadline and free-endpoint scope are superseded.

**Student-budget update:** all model access and quotas are unconfirmed; the current [workflow](../../RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md) treats 1M/model and Astra/Sol/Opus as planning examples. Local protocol/method evidence is primary; any two-role live study scales to verified resources. Two OpenAI models are one developer family. No broad vendor ranking or automatic-extraction reliability claim follows from a small diagnostic.

## Recommendation

Proceed conditionally with **task-relative continuation admission across actual MCP/A2A bridge paths**. The central question should be whether a required caller-visible continuation remains realizable after a specified mapping chain, under explicit capability, handle, scope, timing, and service assumptions. A gate should return supported, unsupported, or unknown, with a checkable reason; it can choose a permitted native path or request an explicit compatible alternative. A general context-repair system, new envelope, handoff success matrix, or standard finite-state checker would not distinguish this project sufficiently.

The contribution is still a candidate. Prior art is substantially closer than the original proposal assumes. The team must earn its novelty claim through an algorithmic distinction and independently grounded results, then reassess at day seven before scaling. A six-week workflow can deliver a bounded defensible paper and artifact; it cannot promise high novelty, conference acceptance, or publication.

## Smallest distinct mechanism worth pursuing

The classical adversarial check is mandatory: [Dijkstra's predicate transformers](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/transcriptions/EWD04xx/EWD472.html), [quotient/residual contracts](https://eskang.github.io/assets/papers/memocode18.pdf), [IR-MIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09035) and [Pacti](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17751) predate this project. Ordinary existential relational preimage already satisfies Pre_exists(F1;F2,Q) = Pre_exists(F1, Pre_exists(F2,Q)). Adding task identities, principals, resource access or expiry to Q does not make that recurrence new. Dijkstra's total-correctness weakest precondition and interface-automata game quantifiers are different from existential may-reachability and must be described accurately.

Compare against bounded product may-reachability with the same runtime-visible IR, contract, evidence and horizon, plus symbolic/backward and credible cached/incremental variants for cost claims. This baseline receives no gold; the separately authored private oracle grades correctness. Compare quotient-based residual obligations on shared expressible fragments. Require a demonstrated advantage beyond standard recurrence or weak-feature counterexamples before retaining C1's algorithmic-novelty claim.

Use a **requirement-driven residual-obligation procedure**, with existing formal methods named explicitly. A contract is a finite set of required continuations and observable conditions, not a complete natural-language understanding of an agent. For example: resume the same pending-input task; retrieve an exact artifact; correlate a reopened handle to the original task; request cancellation without claiming immediate termination; and distinguish unavailable evidence from a fabricated substitute.

For each pinned adapter profile, describe exposed operations, guarded transitions, identity/reference mappings, and assumptions. Propagate the task's remaining requirements through the route, retaining cross-edge dependencies such as the principal that can dereference a returned artifact or the namespace that can resolve a handle. A checklist that separately sees `resume=true`, `retrieve=true`, and `cancel=true` cannot determine whether these operations apply to the same reachable task/resource under the receiving principal.

The result should carry:

1. The required operation and projected route operation.
2. The state/handle/resource relation on which it depends.
3. Conditions discharged by versioned evidence, and unverified conditions.
4. A compatible residual contract, an incompatibility witness, or an unknown reason.
5. A bounded permitted resolution: materialize authorized evidence, select an already permitted native route, or request an explicit alternative contract. Do not invent an unavailable resume operation or silently relax a mandatory requirement.

A supported result needs a constructive bounded continuation certificate or a proved sufficient residual-contract condition checked independently. An empty residual list or advertised operation flags do not establish realizability. An unsupported result needs a valid mandatory-operation/forbidden-requirement witness or a bounded no-continuation certificate under a complete profile/model for the relevant actions. Generic missing advertisements and failed attempts alone do not prove unsupported; incomplete/stale profiles, unobservable conditions, probe failures and checker timeouts can instead require unknown. Witness minimization is reduction-minimal under a declared procedure, not necessarily globally shortest.

Honor explicit normative negative evidence: pinned [A2A section 3.3.4](https://a2a-protocol.org/v1.0.0/specification/) makes absent/false streaming, push-notification and extended-card capabilities non-support for their listed operations. This can establish an operation-level incompatibility; it need not make the whole task unsupported if an agreed polling or other permitted alternative fulfills the contract. Section 3.3.1 makes Send Message idempotency optional, so messageId alone does not establish deduplication or exactly-once effects. Stronger retry guarantees need a declared implementation/application contract and behavioral tests.

These are design recommendations, not established novel mathematics. The candidate algorithm must provide a concrete advantage over feature intersection, per-edge conformance, static contract linting, and whole-product model checking: better false-acceptance/coverage behavior, reusable evidence, cheaper checks, or interpretable dependency witnesses. If no such advantage or substantive new native-bridge finding appears, the deliverable is an applied conformance/replication study.

The primary semantics is **finite may-compatibility**: at least one permitted continuation trace fulfills the contract within the frozen bound. Selected safety properties are evaluated separately over explicitly allowed modeled traces. May-compatibility does not establish universal progress or successful LLM behavior. Stronger strategy-versus-environment progress guarantees are outside the initial six-week scope; there is no primary may/must comparison.

Freeze horizon units (operations and/or elapsed time), starting state, legal actions, principal/resource scope and timing/availability assumptions consistently across gate and oracle. Include preflight, probes and fallback in end-to-end time/call/action/token accounting; revalidate conditions that expire during these steps. Do not compare an oracle with unlimited recovery against a gate with a tighter hidden horizon.

Safe active probes cannot establish all future behavior of an arbitrary opaque service. Supported must mean supported relative to the declared, versioned, tested abstraction and current assumptions; unavailable, stale, insufficient, or unobservable evidence yields unknown. Runtime checks at continuation time are needed where a preflight assumption can expire. A synthetic gold oracle must not be secretly visible to the practical gate.

Similarly, faithful mappings with mutually satisfied composition assumptions cannot all pass a sound preservation check and then violate its theorem. A local-pass/global-fail case must identify which cheaper local check omitted a cross-edge assumption. This is evidence that the local abstraction is inadequate, not a counterexample to correctly stated composition.

## Falsifiable RQs and C1–C3

**RQ1 — Admission validity.** On held-out task/route/state cases, does the requirement-driven gate classify finite may-compatible continuation eligibility more accurately than schema validation, advertised-feature intersection, and per-edge checks? Report false acceptance, false rejection, unknown rate, supported coverage, witness validity, and checking time. Evaluate selected safety assertions separately. Distinguish supplied exact contracts from imperfect runtime-derived contracts.

**RQ2 — Composition and external validity.** Which undeclared assumption interactions appear on independently maintained MCP/A2A bridge paths, and do decisions transfer to an unseen bridge/profile or operation combination? Include all admitted bridges and known documented limits. The presence of an unsupported feature is not automatically a protocol bug or new discovery.

**RQ3 — Practical utility.** With the same observable information and resource budget, does the gate plus permitted fallback improve strict task completion or reduce inappropriate acceptance compared with a strong manifest-aware LLM policy and simpler gates? Measure time, total tokens, permitted actions, refusal cost, and coverage. A system that refuses everything must score zero useful coverage.

Provisional contributions:

- **C1:** A precise finite may-compatible task-relative continuation contract and residual-obligation admission algorithm, with soundness only relative to the frozen abstraction/bound/assumptions and independently checked positive/refusal certificates. The claim is the implemented algorithm and its measured advantage, not a new general contract/composition theorem or universal progress guarantee.
- **C2:** A reproducible executable corpus of native bridge continuation cases and independent finite-reference oracles, including naturally occurring implementation behavior and clearly labeled synthetic fault tests. This supports the method; a benchmark title or additional vendors alone is insufficient.
- **C3:** A sealed empirical study of admission accuracy, composition transfer, and live heterogeneous-agent usefulness, with action/evidence/compute-matched controls and independent artifact reproduction. Claim only the properties, models, and routes actually covered.

## Validation and comparator requirements

The mandatory deterministic comparison should include schema/TCK checks, advertised-feature intersection, per-edge lifecycle checks, an AgentThread-style bounded composition verifier or explicitly justified relevant subset, and an independent complete finite oracle. Include a CONTINUITY-style contract/finality baseline for any authority/freshness headline claims. Incorporate an OCP/Precept-like loss/required-field baseline for content requirements. These are scoped adaptations unless the exact released implementation can be run; never label an imitation as the authors' original system.

For the current small live study, compare native/reference, conventional bridge, strong manifest-aware equal-budget recovery and proposed admission on the same backbone. Simple gates remain local deterministic comparators. PACT-style state and AgentAsk/BRIDGE-informed repair matter where communication-repair superiority is claimed. Do not omit the strongest applicable comparator silently: run it, document a scoped adaptation, or remove the unsupported claim.

Deployment-supplied manually specified contracts are acceptable for the local core when labeled accurately. A reliable automatic-extraction claim needs ordinary workflow documents, sender-visible artifacts, public manifests and measured extraction omissions; the small student diagnostic cannot establish broad reliability. Keep private task/gold state independent. Record supplied contracts, gate decisions, probe budgets, allowed fallback and realized actions.

Evaluate gate correctness against an independent finite oracle for the exact **C_runtime** supplied to the gate. Separately evaluate the complete extraction/admission pipeline against evaluator-private **C_gold**. An omitted gold requirement is an extraction/pipeline failure, not automatically an unsound gate decision on its supplied contract. Both analyses are needed: perfect-contract validity does not establish a reliable practical pipeline, and pipeline failure does not identify which component was wrong.

Use deterministic environment predicates for identity, state, artifact bytes/digests, resource availability, scope, and side effects. Two contributors should independently derive expected traces and review minimized witnesses. The finite oracle must use separately authored state/transition logic; testing a gate against the same implementation renamed as an oracle is circular. Exhaustively cross-check tiny models, then replay their traces through real SDK/wire paths.

For the current budget, use two local domains and 48 held-out authored templates in the core, with 12 live templates by default and 24 only if pilot usage fits. Repetitions over deterministic fixtures show reproducibility and schedule coverage; they do not supply independent statistical samples. Live intervals cluster over templates, retain domain/direction strata and remain coarse. Preserve negative results and restrict generalization accordingly.

Prioritize actual independent public bridges over two mappings authored from the same design. Check code ancestry and shared libraries. An author-created intentionally lossy adapter is a fault injection, not external confirmation. Include documented non-support honestly and require native reference success before interpreting a bridged failure.

Candidate bridge claims about modern MCP Tasks support, durable handles, mapping fidelity and known limitations remain author-documented claims until the pinned code is executed. Their documented limitations are replication baselines; neither those claims nor this research note establishes installation, conformance or a new failure finding.

## Day-seven decision gate for the student team

These steps begin after workflow approval. Research and specification work can precede approval; implementation cannot.

| Day | Concrete evidence | Owner/cross-review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Property-by-property closest-work table; finite may-compatibility and separate safety definitions; frozen bound/assumptions; actual bridge/version/extension eligibility; reject broad first-ever claims | C owns audit; A checks operation semantics; B checks task relevance |
| 3–4 | Direct native reference and at least two independent bridge paths execute a small supported/unsupported/unknown continuation set; expected traces checked against pinned docs | A runs paths; B independently specifies expected outcomes; C audits claims |
| 5–6 | Prototype residual gate and independent tiny oracle; compare schema/features/per-edge checks; minimize one cross-edge assumption case; test at least one honest unknown | A implements gate; B implements oracle; C attacks both/scorer |
| 7 | Written novelty and feasibility decision, with no favorable-result cherry-picking | Student contributors plus an independent peer/supervisor/research mentor |

Go only if all of the following hold:

- The algorithmic distinction survives comparison with AgentThread, CONTINUITY, BRIDGE, existing contract systems, and classical compatibility methods.
- At least two viable independent native bridge implementations can be evaluated under pinned, legitimate profiles.
- A cross-edge, task-relative case has externally grounded behavior and a valid witness beyond a copied documented limitation, or the method demonstrates a meaningful measurable classification/coverage advantage on independent held-out cases.
- Tiny exhaustive oracle checks validate finite may-compatibility, supported/refusal certificates and separate safety assertions. Gate correctness on C_runtime is separated from extraction/pipeline behavior against C_gold, and the practical gate uses only available observable evidence.
- The team has a credible six-week path to sufficiently diverse sealed tasks, matched live controls, and clean reproduction.

If overlap remains complete, pivot to a clearly named replication/conformance systems study or redesign the mechanism before scaling. If only artificial loss creates an effect, keep the synthetic robustness result and withdraw a natural interoperability failure claim. If exact contracts work but runtime extraction fails, make the evidence/unknown gap central rather than leaking gold. If refusal eliminates useful coverage, redesign or report that trade-off. A null result is retained and changes the claim; it is not permission to manufacture novelty.

## Model and publication positioning

Use models actually verified available in one shared runtime; Astra/Sol/Opus are candidate examples, not arranged allowances. No additional family or GPU-hosted model is required for the local core. Pin returned identities/settings and preserve deterministic replay; acknowledge hosted-model drift. Distinguish developer, host and framework, and restrict claims to actual tested coverage. No training-heavy clarifier is necessary.

The most defensible likely paper is a focused software/systems contribution: a task-relative admission method, new native implementation evidence, and measured downstream usefulness. A strong general AI benchmark claim requires correspondingly broad evaluation and cannot follow from a bridge test suite alone. Select a venue after the contribution and evidence are stable, follow its actual scope/reproducibility rules, refresh related work before submission, and avoid acceptance probabilities or guaranteed novelty claims.
