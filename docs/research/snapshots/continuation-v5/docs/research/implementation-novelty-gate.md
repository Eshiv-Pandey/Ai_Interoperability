## Three honest contribution routes

**Method route.** Demonstrate a substantive procedure beyond standard relational preimage, product reachability or contract quotient. A proof of a different property, a useful exact representation, a sound approximation with a defensible evidence boundary, or an evidence acquisition/invalidation method can be candidates. The claim must name its assumptions and limitations and survive implementation on independent routes. Novelty review remains required even if tests pass.

**Efficiency route.** Preserve the declared decision/certificate correctness while reducing a prespecified practical cost. Include optimized whole-route reachability, backward/symbolic computation on the common fragment, and credible cached/incremental alternatives. Measure all cost shifted onto profile construction, probes, proof generation, checking, cache updates and invalidation. A single tiny graph's timing, omission of identity/time predicates or comparing a warm cache to a cold baseline fails this gate. Test changes to task contract, task identity, namespace, principal, profile revision and expiry; unchanged evidence can reuse results, changed dependencies must invalidate them. Require reproducible improvement beyond measurement noise on independently selected cases and more than one nontrivial scale. The team must freeze a meaningful threshold before collecting candidate performance; do not choose it retrospectively.

**Applied systems route.** Withdraw algorithmic novelty and establish a sufficiently substantive, previously undocumented task-relative interoperability finding or reusable verified artifact. Require native success, two independent real mappings, source/wire-backed cross-edge dependency, a near-miss positive control and independent replay. A feature explicitly documented as unsupported is replication. An author-created lossy adapter is an injected robustness case. A finding already reported by the closest work is not new. A narrower artifact may still be useful, but it must be reviewed on its actual contribution rather than inherit C1's novelty claim.

A first-week prototype may show *promising evidence* for one route; that authorizes continued development under the recorded claim. It does not prove the final theorem, generalize beyond evaluated profiles or replace confirmation and independent reproduction.

## Fair-comparator rules

| Comparator | What it establishes and what it cannot establish |
| --- | --- |
| Schema, capability-intersection and per-edge gates | Controls for weaker checks. A dependency they omit explains their failure; beating them does not establish a new whole-route method. |
| **Exact bounded whole-route may-reachability** | Mandatory baseline for the same runtime model and contract. On complete finite evidence an exact solver already has correct binary decisions: a candidate cannot legitimately claim better correctness on that identical problem. Savings, representation, evidence acquisition or a separately defined stronger property are the possible distinctions. |
| Backward/symbolic and cached/incremental variants | Required for checking-cost, residual-obligation or reusable-evidence claims. Give them the same evidence and changes; count initialization and maintenance. Ordinary memoization is prior art. |
| Quotient/assume–guarantee methods | Compare on a common expressible fragment and preserve their actual quantifiers. Do not call existential paths equivalent to demonic weakest preconditions, alternating compatibility or universal safety. |
| AgentThread/CONTINUITY/PC2-informed checks | Include the applicable composition, authorization, freshness or proof-obligation comparator if that property is a headline claim. Use the released system where feasible; label an adapted fragment and remove unsupported superiority claims if it cannot be compared credibly. |
| AgentAsk/BRIDGE-informed equal-budget recovery | Necessary if downstream repair/clarification superiority is claimed. These are conditional live comparators; model access and costs must be verified first. An imitation is an adaptation, not the authors' original implementation. |

The evaluator-private oracle may know independently authored reference state; the candidate and practical comparators may not. Grade admission on the exact supplied runtime contract, then grade extraction plus admission separately against private requirements. More coverage obtained by silently dropping requirements, accepting stale evidence or treating unknown as false fails validity.
